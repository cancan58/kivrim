Gizlilik Politikası

Kıvrım — Support

Problem or idea? Email hcodeveloper@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, iOS version and level.

Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Are ads required? No. An ad only plays when you choose: to continue or to get a hint.

How do I change my ad choice? In Europe, tap the hand icon at the top left of the home screen.
